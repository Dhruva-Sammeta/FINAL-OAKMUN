Timing
When will they drop?
Allocations are published shortly after the registration window closes. Exact dates will be announced on our Instagram and via email.
Notification
How will I be notified?
Your allocation will be sent directly to the email address provided during registration. It will also be listed on this page by delegate name.
What's included
What does my allocation include?
Each delegate receives a committee assignment and a country portfolio. This determines your role, your position paper topic, and your bloc going into the conference.
